# KILO_ARIA_PHASE_L.2_AXUM_MIGRATION_RESEARCH — Axum Research (10 min)

## Objective
Research Axum (Tokio web framework) as a future FastAPI replacement for ARIA backend.

## Background
Video research confirmed:
- Axum is 10-100x faster than FastAPI for certain workloads
- Type-safe extractors provide compile-time request validation
- Tokio ecosystem integrates with Rust memory safety patterns
- WGPU integration planned for v6.0 (Tauri native rendering)
